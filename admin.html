Festive Theme
Changes the colours and adds small decorations on the website. Auto switches Halloween on by itself from 17 Oct to 1 Nov, the Christmas logo ribbon from 1 Dec to 1 Jan, and the April Fools game bar on 1 April, the Pride logo for all of June, and the Earth Day facts screen on 22 April (Nairobi time). Preview any time on the site by adding ?theme=halloween or ?theme=aprilfools or ?theme=pride or ?theme=earthday to the address.
