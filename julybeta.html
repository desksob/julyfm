About
July Radio is a personal 24/7 internet radio station. Running curated playlists, new music discovery, focused listening blocks, and live open air sets around the clock.
Alongside the station, July also streams a 24/7 music video TV channel running in parallel with the audio broadcast. July Radio is also a visual studio, starting with editing work and progressing into music videos and other related productions.
